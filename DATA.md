# Data sources, terms and attribution

Official source records retain their terms. Four portal datasets declare PUBLIC_DOMAIN or USGOV_WORKS. The non-major dataset points to an unknown-licence designation; its reuse rights require verification before public redistribution. Official attribution identifies the Federal Transit Administration. The original metadata files preserve the stated public-domain link and public access designation. Code licensing does not replace source terms. Attribution: Federal Transit Administration, U.S. Department of Transportation, National Transit Database. No endorsement is implied.

CSV files retain all fields from complete reconciled exports. The major-event convenience CSV is a lossless normalised concatenation of frozen official pages, with public row identities checked for uniqueness. Its constituent page URLs, counts and hashes are also retained. Stored gzip streams are deterministic and decompress to the original CSV bytes; both hashes and byte sizes are recorded. Counts are reconciled to the source count endpoint. Before/after metadata confirms that rowsUpdatedAt did not change during each accepted acquisition. This is a consistency check, not an atomic snapshot guarantee. Release timestamps are portal rowsUpdatedAt, not event dates or formal certification dates. Official explanatory attachments retain their source terms.

Distribution: major-event and FRA incident exports and major-event pages remain local and untracked because they contain sensitive incident details. Non-major rows remain local and untracked because redistribution rights are unresolved. Original metadata also remains local because its cached samples contain source values; data/catalog retains catalogue descriptions and schemas with cached values removed. The manifest records original hashes, sizes and distribution status. Incident/non-major record-level join logs, full quality issue logs and revision changes remain local and untracked. Released derived tables contain no incident narratives, person types, addresses or coordinates. Field-completeness tables contain only field names and aggregate counts.

## Coverage and unresolved acquisition

FTA workbook and original manual downloads returned access errors in the direct route; the browser workbook download did not produce a verified file. The current portal exports are complete, but the safety records unique to the unacquired 2002-2013 workbooks and raw unadjusted exposure workbook are not frozen here. Two overlapping historical releases were not obtained, so empirical revision rates are unavailable. A fresh export of the same release is not a historical release. Actual submission timestamps are not published in these inputs, so possible late service is a missingness proxy. The timeline uses conservative annual guards where exact implementation dates remain unverified. The public release followed an internal release check on 2026-10-11.
